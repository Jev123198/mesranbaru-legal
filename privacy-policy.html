Kenapa aplikasi ini dibuat

Supaya pelanggan bisa memesan dari ponsel, dan toko bisa menyiapkan barangnya lebih cepat. Begitu pesanan masuk, karyawan langsung mengambil dan mengemasnya — jadi saat pelanggan sampai, barangnya sudah siap.

Semua data di halaman ini dikumpulkan untuk itu saja: menerima pesanan, menyiapkan barangnya, dan menuliskan fakturnya. Tidak untuk iklan, tidak untuk dijual.

Data yang kami kumpulkan

Akun dibuatkan pemilik toko, jadi data awal Anda berasal dari catatan pelanggan toko, bukan dari formulir yang Anda isi.

Tidak kami kumpulkan: lokasi, kontak, SMS, panggilan, kalender, mikrofon, data kartu atau rekening, daftar aplikasi lain, dan data apa pun untuk iklan.

Untuk apa dipakai

Menjalankan aplikasi dan mengelola akun Anda; menerima pesanan, menyiapkan barangnya, dan menuliskan fakturnya; mengirim notifikasi tentang pesanan, faktur, dan produk baru; serta menjaga keamanan akun. Notifikasi boleh Anda tolak atau matikan kapan saja — aplikasi tetap berjalan normal.

Dasarnya adalah hubungan jual beli antara Anda dan toko. Kami tidak memakai data Anda untuk pengambilan keputusan otomatis atau pembentukan profil.

Siapa yang bisa melihat data Anda

Pelanggan lain tidak dapat melihat data Anda. Pembatasan ini dipasang di server (Row Level Security pada basis data), bukan hanya di tampilan aplikasi, sehingga tidak bisa ditembus dengan mengutak-atik aplikasi.

  • Anda — hanya profil, keranjang, favorit, pesanan, dan faktur milik Anda.
  • Pelanggan lain — tidak ada sama sekali.
  • Pemilik toko — data setiap pelanggan, sebagaimana toko mengetahuinya dari buku penjualannya.
  • Tanpa akun — hanya katalog produk dan informasi toko.

Penyedia layanan

Kami tidak menjual dan tidak menukar data Anda. Kami hanya memakai:

  • Supabase — basis data, autentikasi, penyimpanan berkas.
  • Expo dan Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — pengiriman notifikasi.

Mereka memproses data atas perintah kami dan tidak boleh memakainya untuk tujuan sendiri. Server mereka berada di luar Indonesia. Kami juga dapat membuka data bila diwajibkan hukum.

Keamanan dan penyimpanan

  • Semua hubungan aplikasi dan server memakai enkripsi HTTPS/TLS.
  • Kata sandi disimpan teracak dan tidak dapat dibaca siapa pun, termasuk pemilik toko.
  • Percobaan masuk dengan kata sandi salah berulang kali dikunci sementara.
  • Profil, pesanan, dan faktur disimpan selama akun aktif; keranjang dan favorit sampai Anda menghapusnya.

Tidak ada sistem yang kebal sepenuhnya. Bila terjadi kebocoran data pribadi, kami akan memberitahukan pihak terdampak dan pihak berwenang sesuai ketentuan.

Hak Anda

Anda berhak melihat, memperbaiki, meminta salinan, dan menghapus data pribadi Anda, serta menarik persetujuan. Nama, nomor telepon, dan email kontak dapat Anda ubah sendiri di Akun → Edit Profil.

Untuk permintaan lain, hubungi atau WhatsApp . Kami menanggapi paling lambat 30 hari. Cara menghapus akun ada di halaman Permintaan Hapus Akun.

Anak-anak dan perubahan

Aplikasi ini untuk pelanggan dewasa 18 tahun ke atas; kami tidak mengumpulkan data anak-anak. Bila kebijakan ini berubah, tanggal “Diperbarui” di atas kami ubah dan versi terbaru diterbitkan di halaman ini; untuk perubahan penting kami memberitahu lewat aplikasi atau WhatsApp.

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective · Updated · MesranBaru (com.jev123.MesranBaru)

At a glance

  • For customers of Toko Mesran Baru Motor Sengkang only. The shop creates every account — there is no self sign-up.
  • No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics. We do not sell your data.
  • No payments and no prices in the app. We store no card or bank details.
  • Each customer can see only their own data.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS, or calendar.

Who we are

Toko Mesran Baru Motor Sengkang, a motorcycle spare-parts shop in Sengkang, Wajo Regency, South Sulawesi, Indonesia. We are the data controller for the data described here, under Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

Why this app was built

So customers can order parts from their phone, and the shop can have them ready sooner. As soon as an order arrives the staff pick and pack it — so by the time the customer reaches the shop, their parts are waiting.

Everything described here is collected for that alone: taking orders, preparing them, and writing the invoice. Not for advertising, and not to be sold.

What we collect

The shop owner creates each account, so your initial details come from the shop's own customer records rather than a form you filled in.

Your login session is kept on your device. Our hosting provider logs IP addresses and request times for security. The shop account can also take product photos and upload a stock file — neither feature exists on customer accounts.

We do not collect: location, contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar, microphone, card or bank details, the list of your installed apps, or anything for advertising.

How we use it

To run the app and manage your account; to take orders, prepare them, and write invoices; to send notifications about orders, invoices, and new products; and to keep accounts secure. You may decline or disable notifications at any time — the app works normally without them.

The basis is the trading relationship between you and the shop. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who can see your data

Other customers cannot see your data. This is enforced on the server (database Row Level Security), not merely in the app's screens, so it cannot be bypassed by tampering with the app.

  • You — only your own profile, cart, favourites, orders, and invoices.
  • Other customers — nothing at all.
  • The shop owner — every customer's details, as the shop already knows them from its sales records.
  • Without an account — only the product catalogue and shop information.

Service providers

We do not sell or trade your data. We use only:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — notification delivery.

They process data on our instructions only and may not use it for their own purposes. Their servers are outside Indonesia. We may also disclose data where required by law.

Security and retention

  • All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored hashed and cannot be read by anyone, including the shop owner.
  • Repeated wrong-password attempts temporarily lock an account.
  • Profile, orders, and invoices are kept while the account is active; cart and favourites until you remove them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected individuals and the relevant authority as required by law.

Your rights

You may access, correct, request a copy of, and delete your personal data, and withdraw consent. Your name, phone number, and contact email can be changed yourself under Account → Edit Profile.

For anything else, contact or WhatsApp . We respond within 30 days. Deletion instructions are on the Account Deletion page.

Children and changes

This app is for business customers aged 18 and over; we do not knowingly collect children's data. If this policy changes we will update the “Updated” date above and publish the new version here; for significant changes we will also notify you in the app or by WhatsApp.
